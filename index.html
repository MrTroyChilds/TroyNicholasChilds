About

A clear record of who I am and the work I do.

I come from environments where systems had to work under pressure — not just look good on paper. My experience includes Army 31U communications, tactical radio systems, networked equipment, system troubleshooting, deployment, maintenance, and resolving problems in demanding field environments.

From the Rifleman Radio lab and field build with General Dynamics C4 Systems to fiber optic backbone and fusion splice training on the SH-130 corridor in Texas, I consistently bring value and technical competence to the teams I am attached to.
